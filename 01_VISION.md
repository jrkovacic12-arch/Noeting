# Noeting — Vision and Principles

> **Status:** Foundational direction from the original note, expanded by the user's Obsidian experience and personal-agent ambition.
>
> **Motto:** **Notice. Note. Think. Originate from nothing.**

## 1. Origin

The original Noeting question was whether seemingly meaningful content products actually improve attention when their format trains continuous consumption. Useful content is not equivalent to useful attention. The desired progression is:

**Original source → own notes → reflection → occasional summary → creation.**

The defining distinction is **sustained thought vs. repeated consumption**, not simply long-form vs. short-form media. Noeting should protect internally generated ideas without being another environment that captures attention.

A practical rule: **Capture the thought briefly, then return. Develop it later.**

The authentic, unchanged founding account is preserved in [archive/noeting-original.md](archive/noeting-original.md).

## 2. Expanded purpose

Obsidian usage revealed a second need beyond reflection: the user captures plans and to-dos, reviews them later, and wants help with organization, daily synthesis, priorities, and reminders. Unclear competing commitments can cause overwhelm.

**Working vision:** A privacy-first, local-first personal thinking **and action** system that transforms captured information into clarity, considered decisions, and meaningful work — without training dependence on the app.

The long-term ambition is a personal AI agent. This is a direction, **not** a claim that autonomy or integrations are required for the initial version.

## 3. Two connected loops

### Thinking loop

**Notice → Capture → Reflect → Connect → Experiment/Create → Revisit **

Preserve thought that arises from life, conversations, mistakes, observations, work, books, papers, films, the web, and spontaneous associations. Make reflection and original output more important than accumulating content.

### Action loop

**Capture → Clarify → Prioritize → Act → Review **

Separate actionable commitments from interesting ideas. Surface a manageable set of priorities, clarify what can wait, and support follow-through without manufacturing new obligations.

**How the loops reinforce each other:** Thinking can clarify what is worth doing; action can test an idea, change circumstances, or produce new evidence for reflection. The loops are independently useful, with voluntary connections between them: a thought might inspire an experiment, an experiment might become an accepted task, and a completed task might inform a later reflection or creation.

**Neither loop is mandatory for the other.** A note may remain a note; an obligation may be completed without reflection; understanding or changing one's mind can be a meaningful outcome without a task. Noeting should support these transitions without pressuring users to convert every thought into productivity or treating every completed task as a prompt to stay in the app. Concrete UX remains subject to validation in [02_PRODUCT.md](02_PRODUCT.md) and [07_V1_WORKFLOW_DRAFT.md](07_V1_WORKFLOW_DRAFT.md).

## 4. Core principles

1. **Attention sovereignty.** User intention, not an algorithmic feed, should determine the next action.
2. **Capture with minimal interruption.** Saving should be fast; a quick capture should not automatically open a chat, feed, or recommendation stream.
3. **Creation over accumulation.** Information becomes more useful through interpretation, experiments, and original work.
4. **Clarity over activity.** Task management should reduce the number of competing priorities, not just create a longer list.
5. **User-directed sessions.** Today/review remain finite views of saved records. No deliberate-stop control, completion screen, or closing prompt is required; users close the app themselves. This supersedes the earlier explicit-stop design following phone feedback on 2026-10-13 (D-012).
6. **Privacy, autonomy, portability.** User data is primarily local; external services and agent access should be optional, bounded, understandable, and reversible.
7. **AI is instrumental, not authoritative.** AI may assist with organizing, synthesis, planning, or critique; it must not silently decide personal commitments or alter source notes.
8. **Real life is the primary environment.** The app should help users return to reading, working, living, and creating.

## 5. AI: permitted help and important tension

The founding concept favors AI **behind deliberate user actions**; the expanded needs raise the possibility of automated categorization and daily summaries. Reconciling those aims remains a design question, not a finalized policy.

See [P-001 and P-004](05_DECISIONS.md) for proposal status, [02_PRODUCT.md](02_PRODUCT.md) for user-facing behavior, and [03_ARCHITECTURE.md](03_ARCHITECTURE.md) for technical/privacy enforcement.

## 6. What Noeting is not

Noeting is **not** primarily:

- A book-summaries feed or reading-content platform.
- A system of streaks, points, trending content, follower counts, or engagement metrics.
- A general-purpose knowledge graph users must spend hours organizing.
- An Anki clone; resurfacing should ask **“What do you think now?”**, not only “Can you remember this?”.
- A fully autonomous agent that acts without understandable scope or permissions.
- A replacement for the user's own thought, judgment, or responsibility.

Potential later concepts from the original note (not MVP commitments): intention gates, a self-chosen experience queue, more advanced synthesis, and desktop/sync.

## 7. Success criteria at the level of values

Progress should be judged by whether Noeting helps someone:

- Capture an insight without losing the original activity.
- Recognize their actual commitments and select a realistic next action.
- Reflect on how their thinking changes over time.
- Produce or test something original.
- Choose when to leave the app without a required closing flow.

Do **not** optimize primarily for daily active use, session length, number of notes, AI messages, or time in app. The question remains: **What behavior does this repeatedly train?**

## 8. Identity and scope statement

**Identity:** *Notice. Note. Think. Originate from nothing.*

**Practical product direction:** A deliberate personal system for **capturing, understanding, organizing, prioritizing, acting, learning from experience, and reflecting**.

These statements are complementary: the first protects the philosophy; the second defines the added task-management need.
